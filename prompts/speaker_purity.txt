Listen to the audio clip directly.
Evaluate whether the audio contains exclusively ONE speaker or multiple voices.

Categories:
- "pure": Exactly one voice is heard from beginning to end. Zero background whispers, laughter, or background conversations.
- "secondary_speaker": A second speaker is heard at any point.
- "overlapping_speech": Two or more people speaking or reacting at the same time.

DECISION:
- "pass": If speaker_purity is "pure".
- "reject": If speaker_purity is "secondary_speaker" or "overlapping_speech".

Return strict JSON only:
{
  "speaker_purity": "pure" | "secondary_speaker" | "overlapping_speech",
  "decision": "pass" | "reject",
  "reason": "Brief explanation of who is heard."
}
