Use single key presses when this tab is active to audition and label speech turns at high speed:

1 Accept — Mark turn as clean single-speaker speech suitable for TTS (auto-advances if enabled).
2 Background Noise — Toggle defect tag: music, hum, static, or ambient noise.
3 >1 Speaker — Toggle defect tag: overlap, crosstalk, secondary vocal bleed.
4 Word Chopped Off — Toggle defect tag: cut-off ending or start, truncated syllables.
2, 3, 4 Multi-Label — Press any combination of defect keys or click buttons to assign multiple defects simultaneously!
