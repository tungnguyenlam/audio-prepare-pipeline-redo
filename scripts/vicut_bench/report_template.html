Exact Defect Operator Formulations

Defect Operator Formula Severities Hypothesis / Acoustic Significance
Clipped Word End t_cut = speech_offset - Δt 10, 20, 40, 80 ms Final unreleased stop codas (-p/-t/-c/-ch) will have higher perceptual/LLM detection sensitivity than open codas.
Clipped Word Start t_cut = speech_onset + Δt 10, 20, 40, 80 ms Initial acoustic burst and voicing onset clipped; models notice once first phoneme is destroyed.
Secondary Speaker Target + Donor × 10^(-SIR/20) SIR 24, 18, 12, 6 dB Overlapped turns or speaker bleeding from diarization errors.
Background Music Target + Music × 10^(-SNR/20) SNR 30, 24, 18, 12 dB Additive background accompaniment bed leakage from podcast or video.
